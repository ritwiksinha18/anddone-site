Take a closer look.

  • From summary to post. Open a finished run, ride, walk or session and it is already a card. Shape it, then send a still, or a video with the route and the graph drawing as they happen.

  • Your week, synced. Workouts arrive from Apple Health on their own, each carrying the shape of its route. Nothing to import, nothing to log.

  • Routes with depth. Not a flat line on a flat map. Shaders give the route light and depth, and there are enough of them that the choice says something about you.

  • Where it happened. Your route on a real map, satellite or plain, light or dark, and flown over in 3D.

  • Heart rate as drawing. Indoors there is no route, so your heart rate becomes the drawing: the session as one line, its peaks and lows marked, still or moving.

  • Old language, new screen. The micro-graphics templates: thin rules, small type, figures packed tight. More on the card, in less room.

  • Blur, glass, light. Your photographs get the same care as the data. Blur carries the speed, glass holds the numbers, and the route draws itself in light.

  • Every layer, yours. Everything on the card is a layer you can move and resize. Choose the stats, the colours and the look.

  • Image or video. Rendered at the size the destination wants, not the size of your screen. Then it is in your gallery, on your story, or already sent.
